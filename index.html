Three Ways to Work Together

Coaching & consulting for PMs at every stage

01

Career & Interview Coaching

For aspiring and job-searching PMs. We build a story that actually lands — a clear "tell me about yourself," a bank of interview stories with real structure behind them, and a way to talk about impact even when the hard numbers aren't handed to you. We run mock interviews under real pressure, sharpen your resume and positioning, and — if your background doesn't look like a "traditional" PM path — turn that into the most memorable part of your story instead of something to explain away.

02

Growing in the Seat

For PMs who are employed but stretched — the only product person on the team, newly solo, or navigating a manager who isn't giving them what they need. This is about managing up, structuring your own 1:1s, building technical and stakeholder credibility fast in an unfamiliar domain, and telling team opinion apart from what the market is actually telling you.

03

Product & AI Consulting

About

Hi, I'm Liping.

I founded Calm Horizons LLC after stepping away from full-time corporate product management. Before that, I spent over a decade in PM leadership at companies like Morningstar and Avalara, including hands-on experience building GenAI-powered products — most recently as a Senior Product Manager at Payscale and a Manager of Product Management at Avalara, where I led teams delivering self-service onboarding and support experiences used by thousands of customers.

Now I coach and consult with both established and aspiring PMs on career growth, interview prep, and product craft — while also exploring meditation, life coaching, and content creation as part of a broader mission: helping people feel more grounded, and live more meaningful lives.

The short version: I've sat on both sides of the table — as the PM being interviewed and promoted, and as the manager doing the interviewing and promoting. I bring both the operating rigor of a decade in product, and the plain honesty of someone who recently made a hard, deliberate career change herself.

How I Coach

Operating rigor, and room to be honest

01
